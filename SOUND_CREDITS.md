# MCI game sound credits

The following recordings were edited, trimmed, converted to MP3, and mixed into the game. Their source pages describe them as public domain or CC0:

- `siren.mp3`: “Whelen.ogg” by Heggyhomolit, CC0. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Whelen.ogg
- `patient_moan_1.mp3`, `patient_moan_2.mp3`, `patient_moan_3.mp3`: excerpts from “Male pain grunts.ogg” by stilgar, public domain. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Male_pain_grunts.ogg
- `scene_ambience.mp3`: distant voices from “Group discussion 1.ogg” by ezwa, public domain, mixed with an original synthetic room/road bed. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Group_discussion_1.ogg

`vehicle_engine.mp3`, `radio_chirp.mp3`, `footstep.mp3`, and `treatment_gear.mp3` were generated for this MCI patch. The recordings are simulations for training and are not patient data.
